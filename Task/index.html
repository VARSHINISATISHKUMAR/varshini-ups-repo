The Lion

Category: Mammals

Lions are large wild cats known for their strength and social behaviour. They usually live in groups called prides. Lions mainly inhabit parts of Africa, with a small population of wild lions also living in India.

Interesting Facts

  • Lions are carnivores.
  • Male lions often have manes.
  • Lions communicate through roars and other sounds.

The Peacock

Category: Birds

Peacocks are famous for their colourful feathers. Male peafowl spread their large, eye-spotted tail feathers during courtship displays.

Interesting Facts

  • Peafowl belong to the pheasant family.
  • They can fly short distances.
  • The Indian peafowl is India's national bird.

The Green Sea Turtle

Category: Reptiles

Green sea turtles are marine reptiles that spend most of their lives in the ocean. Adult green sea turtles mainly eat seagrasses and algae.

Interesting Facts

  • They breathe air using lungs.
  • Females lay eggs on sandy beaches.
  • They face threats from pollution and habitat loss.
